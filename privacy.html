Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 19, 2026

FocusTube respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how FocusTube handles information when you use the application.

1. Google Account Sign-In

FocusTube allows users to sign in using their Google account. Authentication is provided through Google's authentication services.

FocusTube does not request or store your Google account password.

2. YouTube Data

When you authorize FocusTube, the application may access the YouTube data required to provide its features, including information related to your YouTube account and channels you are subscribed to.

FocusTube uses the YouTube Data API to provide the application's viewing features.

3. How Information Is Used

Information accessed through Google and YouTube is used only to provide the features of FocusTube, including authentication and displaying relevant YouTube content.

4. Data Sharing

FocusTube does not sell your personal information. FocusTube does not use your Google or YouTube data for advertising.

Data is accessed through Google and YouTube services according to the permissions granted by the user.

5. Data Storage

FocusTube is designed to use authentication information only as necessary to provide the application features. Users can sign out of FocusTube at any time.

6. Third-Party Services

FocusTube uses services provided by Google and YouTube. Their services are governed by their own privacy policies and terms.

7. Revoking Access

You can revoke FocusTube's access to your Google account through your Google Account security settings.

8. Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when necessary. Any changes will be published on this page.

9. Contact

For questions about this Privacy Policy or FocusTube, please contact the developer using the contact information provided in the application's Google OAuth configuration.
